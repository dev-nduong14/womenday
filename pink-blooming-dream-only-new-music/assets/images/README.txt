THEM ANH CHO WEBSITE
====================
1. Copy anh JPG/PNG/WEBP vao thu muc nay, vi du: anh-me.jpg
2. Mo assets/js/content.js
3. Tim danh sach GALLERY, dien "file: 'anh-me.jpg'" vao mot muc.
4. Luu, commit, push len GitHub. Website tu hien thi anh do.

Neu ban chua co anh, website hien khung YOUR PHOTO HERE (khong thay anh bang anh stock).
Co the click "Them anh cua ban" tren giao dien de xem thu, tuy nhien anh chi ton tai trong phien trinh duyet do.
